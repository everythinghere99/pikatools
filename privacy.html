Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At PikaTools, accessible from https://everythinghere99.github.io/pikatools/, the privacy of our visitors is one of our extreme priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by PikaTools and how we use it.

1. File Processing and Retention

PikaTools is designed to keep file handling limited to what is necessary to provide each requested tool. Some tools can process files directly in your browser using JavaScript and browser APIs. Other tools may require server-side processing for the requested operation. We do not claim that every PikaTools tool is exclusively client-side. Where a tool requires a file to be sent to a processing service, the file is used to perform the requested operation and is not intended to be retained longer than necessary, subject to the technical operation of that service.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, PikaTools may use cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type or other device parameters.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertisers

Google is one of our third-party vendors. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

PikaTools's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. PikaTools does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent and Contact

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. If you have questions about privacy or file handling, contact us at everythinghere99@gmail.com.
